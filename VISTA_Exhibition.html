02 · Project Relevance

Why this matters to an engineering college

Instructional time

Manual roll-call takes 5–10 minutes per session in a class of 60–70.

VISTA marks a 60-face group photo in 0.683 s on a CPU.

Proxy attendance

Paper registers cannot detect it.

Face matching plus heuristic liveness checks flag blurred or tiny faces.

VTU 75% rule and CIE policy

Eligibility is tracked in spreadsheets, after the fact.

VISTA computes attendance eligibility and CIE 3 eligibility live per subject.

Cost and privacy

RFID/biometric hardware costs ₹50,000–₹2,00,000 per lab. Cloud face APIs send biometrics to third parties.

VISTA runs on standard hardware, on-premise, with an offline rule-based coach fallback (DPDP Act 2023 concern).

05 · Problem Statement

Attendance and risk tracking do not scale by hand

Today

  • Roll-call eats 5–10 minutes per class.
  • Proxy attendance goes unseen.
  • 75% eligibility is found out at semester end.
  • Hardware biometrics are costly and one-at-a-time.
  • Cloud APIs move student biometrics off-campus.

Needed

A software-only, on-premise system that marks a whole classroom from one photograph, warns early about at-risk students, enforces CIE policy automatically, and runs on ordinary institutional hardware.
