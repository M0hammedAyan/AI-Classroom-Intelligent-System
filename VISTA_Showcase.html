05 · Measured results

Numbers, with their limits

The classifier numbers come from small, clean, closed-set test crops. Rejection of unknown (non-enrolled) faces was not tested. Real classrooms with varied pose and lighting will score lower. The XGBoost risk model (macro F1 0.3286, trained on synthetic labels) is below the 53.5% majority baseline, so the rule engine decides risk and SHAP only explains.
