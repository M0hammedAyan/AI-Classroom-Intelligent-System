[3] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: Educational institutions maintain attendance records and assessment results to monitor student participation and academic progress. However, these records are often collected and reviewed through separate processes. Teachers may record attendance manually, enter examination marks in spreadsheets, and identify students requiring support only after reviewing accumulated records. This separation increases administrative effort and makes it difficult to obtain a timely, consolidated view of student performance.
[4] P style=Body Text al=None None,None,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: Computer vision provides a means of automating attendance through face detection and recognition, while data analysis and machine learning can support the identification of academic risk. Integrating these capabilities with an academic management platform allows attendance and assessment records to support both routine administration and student guidance.
[5] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: VISTA, the Visual Intelligence System for Tracking and Analysis, is developed as an integrated platform for face recognition-based attendance, academic record management, and student risk assessment. The system combines a computer vision module, a Fast API backend, a React frontend, and an academic analysis module. Its primary user workspaces support administrators, teachers, and students.
[6] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: The attendance module processes classroom photographs to detect and identify enrolled students. It uses SCRFD face detection and Arc Face facial embeddings through Insight Face, with a trained ResNet-50 classifier as a secondary identification path. Heuristic liveness checks provide an additional screening mechanism. Teachers can review recognition results and manually correct attendance when necessary.
[7] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: The academic analysis component evaluates attendance patterns and assessment performance to identify students who may require support. It includes rule-based risk scoring and a separate machine learning prediction path using XG Boost, with SHAP-based explanations. The platform also incorporates Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) analysis and an AI Study Coach that provides guidance based on recorded academic information.
[8] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: VISTA brings these functions together to support attendance administration, academic monitoring, and informed intervention. Its outputs are intended to assist teachers and students while retaining human review for academic decisions.

[146] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: These findings support evaluating attendance capture, academic records, analytical outputs, and student guidance as related but distinct components.

[149] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: The reviewed literature establishes existing approaches to face-based attendance, administrative record management, classroom engagement analysis, and academic prediction. The practical integration requirement addressed by VISTA is to connect selected functions within a common academic workflow.
[150] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: VISTA combines classroom image processing with teacher review, subject-wise attendance records, assessment information, CIE calculations, academic risk indicators, and student guidance. Its purpose is to make these records available to the users responsible for maintaining and acting on them.
[151] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: The project also distinguishes between operational rules and experimental prediction. Rule-based risk indicators and trained-model outputs are maintained as separate analytical paths. This prevents their results from being presented as one uniformly validated prediction mechanism.
[152] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: Another requirement concerns the usefulness of academic information to students. Attendance percentages and assessment marks describe the current position but do not necessarily explain the next action. VISTA addresses this through attendance recovery calculations, absence allowances, improvement eligibility checks, and required-score estimates.
[153] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: These requirements define the scope of the proposed integration. They do not imply that every reviewed system lacks these functions or that similar combinations are absent from all previous research.

[163] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: Face size, lighting, camera angle, pose, and occlusion can influence recognition outcomes. Classroom evaluation therefore requires representative images rather than relying entirely on prepared facial samples [24].

[165] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: A detected face must be associated with an enrolled student before a confirmed attendance entry is created. Incorrect matches and unsuccessful recognition require review mechanisms.
[166] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,14.0,b=True imgs=[] fld=[]: 2.7.3 Attendance Session Consistency
[167] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: The meaning of an attendance session must remain consistent across capture, storage, correction, and percentage calculation. Inconsistent counting can affect attendance summaries even when identity recognition is correct.
[168] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,14.0,b=True imgs=[] fld=[]: 2.7.4 Academic Data Quality
[169] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: Predictive analysis depends on the completeness and meaning of its inputs. Missing assessment results, inconsistent attendance records, and differences between student populations can reduce the usefulness of academic predictions.

[178] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: The reviewed studies use different datasets, operating environments, recognition methods, and output definitions. Their results therefore cannot support a universal accuracy claim for automated attendance or academic prediction.
[179] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: Publication titles and implementation descriptions also provide different levels of evidence from documented experiments. [180] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: Attendance automation, emotion monitoring, and academic prediction require separate interpretation. [181] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: These limitations motivate a component-wise evaluation of VISTA, with clear distinctions between measured results, calculated estimates, demonstration records, and expected policy outcomes.

[184] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: Future work can extend the reviewed approaches through:

[189] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=True imgs=[] fld=[]: Class-wise performance analysis: Examine less frequent risk categories and compare trained models with simple baselines.
[190] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=True imgs=[] fld=[]: Complete workflow verification: Check the connection between recognition, student identity, attendance sessions, database updates, and displayed results.

[460] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: SCRFD identifies face locations within the image. Insight Face also provides the facial information required for Arc Face embedding extraction.

[470] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: The classifier can identify only the classes represented in its trained artifact.

[922] P style=Normal al=JUSTIFY (3) Times New Roman,12.0,b=None imgs=[] fld=[]: VISTA processes facial images, identity-linked embeddings, attendance records, and assessment information. Consequently, this report does not claim that VISTA has completed a legal compliance assessment. Deployment requires review against the provisions and rules applicable at that time.
